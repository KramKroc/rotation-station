# Rotation Station

Fair minutes and subs for youth basketball coaches. A free phone app that plans who plays when, so every player gets fair minutes, tells you when to sub, and keeps the score and fouls during the game.

**Open the app:** https://kramkroc.github.io/rotation-station/
**How to use it:** https://kramkroc.github.io/rotation-station/guide.html

## Install
- **iPhone / iPad:** open the link in Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome → ⋮ → *Install app*.

Open it once with signal; after that it works offline. The [guide](https://kramkroc.github.io/rotation-station/guide.html) covers setting up your squad, game day, sharing with an assistant coach, and what to do if something goes wrong.

## Privacy
No account, no tracking, no server. Everything (names, minutes, scores) is stored only in the browser on your phone. Nothing leaves the phone unless you copy it out yourself.
